# Mailing lists

Who is on each opt-in mailing list, and in what state. Each list shows how many addresses are waiting to confirm, have confirmed, or have left.

## What you can do here

- **Pick a list** to see its subscribers, 50 per page.
- **Filter** by state, or **search** by email address.
- **Send confirmation again** to an address that signed up but has not confirmed yet.
- **Unsubscribe** an address. This works exactly like the person using the unsubscribe link in an email: the opt-out and the consent record are kept.
- **Export confirmed (CSV)**: download the confirmed addresses with their language, when they confirmed, and the account they belong to where there is one.

## States

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Waiting to confirm | Signed up, but has not clicked the confirmation link yet. Receives no list mail. |
| Confirmed | Confirmed the subscription. Receives the list's mail. |
| Left | Unsubscribed. |

## Good to know

- You cannot add anyone here. An opt-in list holds only people who asked to be on it, with proof that they did. Adding an address by hand would put someone on the list without their consent.
- Only addresses waiting to confirm can be sent the confirmation again.
- The export includes confirmed addresses only.
- If no list appears, the site has no opt-in list registered yet. Lists are declared by the application, not created on this page.

## Common questions

- **Someone asks to be added.** Send them to the site's sign-up form, or ask them to subscribe themselves, so their consent is recorded.
- **Someone never got the confirmation.** Use Send confirmation again, then check the Email log for that address.
