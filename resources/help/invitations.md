# Invitations

Invite someone to create an account by email, and keep track of who has been invited. An invitation is a one-time link: the person follows it and sets up their account.

## What you can do here

- **Invite somebody**: enter their email address, and optionally an organisation, a role and a note. The link is emailed to them.
- See every invitation with its state, when it was sent and when it expires.
- **Resend** a waiting or expired invitation. A new link is emailed and the previous link stops working.
- **Withdraw** an invitation that has not been used. Its link stops working at once.

## Good to know

- The link is shown on screen **once**, right after you create or resend it. Copy it then if you want to hand it over another way, for example when the email is slow or filtered. It cannot be shown again later, because only a fingerprint of it is stored.
- If the email could not be queued, the page says so and shows the link for you to pass on yourself.
- A link works once, only for the invited address, and only until it expires. The page shows how many hours a link lasts.
- The role option only appears if you are allowed to assign roles. A role given by invitation is a normal role assignment.
- Used and withdrawn invitations cannot be resent; invite the address again instead.

## Common questions

- **The person says the link does not work.** It may have expired, been used already, or been replaced by a resend. Resend it and ask them to use the newest email.
