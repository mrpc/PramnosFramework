# Users

All the accounts on the site. Find a person, look at their account, and act on it: edit details, lock or unlock, reset a password, send a message, or see their sessions and tokens.

## What you can do here

- **Search** the list; each column has its own filter. Click the id or username to open an account.
- **+ New User** creates an account.
- On an account's page: see its details, status, statistics, recent tokens, roles and email preferences.
- **Edit User** changes the account's details, password, user type, per-user settings and direct permissions.
- **Lock Account** stops the person signing in; **Unlock Account** lets them in again.
- **Send a Message** by email, in-app notification or push. Only the channels the account can actually receive are offered.
- **Sessions** shows where the person is signed in now. **All Tokens** lists their application tokens.
- Depending on the site's theme, the page also offers **Send password reset**, **Clear lockout** after failed sign-ins, turning off **two-factor** for someone who lost their phone, revoking a lost **passkey**, switching **new sign-in alerts**, and the full **activity** history.
- **User types** explains what each type of account may do by default.

## Good to know

- Accounts are never erased from here. Deleting or locking switches the account off and keeps its history.
- You cannot give anyone a higher user type than your own, nor edit an account that ranks above you.
- Turning off someone's second factor, sending a message and changing their email preferences are recorded in the account's activity log.
- Turning an opt-in mailing list *on* for someone sends them a confirmation email; they must confirm it themselves.
- What a person may do also depends on their roles, groups and user type, not only on what is set on their own page.
