# Mass messages

Compose and send one message to many accounts at once, by email or other channels, and follow how the delivery is going.

## What you can do here

- **See every message**, newest first, with how many were delivered, failed or are still pending.
- **Compose** a message: a subject, a body, the channel, and the audience. You can save it as a draft and come back to it.
- **Choose the audience** by user type range, language, groups, organisations, sign-in dates, second-factor use, or specific accounts to include or exclude. Or send to a mailing list instead.
- **Preview the audience** before saving or sending, to see how many accounts the criteria match.
- **Schedule** the message with a send time, and for email choose the options: the wrapper (design), preheader and unsubscribe list.
- **Send** the message, which queues it for delivery.
- **View** a sent message and its recipients, and open **tracking** to see who opened it, who clicked, and which links were followed.
- **Delete** a message that was never sent.

## Good to know

- Sending means queueing. The recipients are recorded at once and a background task delivers them over the next minutes, so progress grows gradually. If nothing moves, check the Services and Queue pages.
- A message can be sent only once. Once it has recipients it cannot be edited, sent again or deleted, because it is the record of what people received.
- Always check the audience count before sending. A wrong audience cannot be taken back.
- A mailing list can only be sent an email.
- Use "Exclude anyone who unsubscribed from" so people who opted out of that kind of mail do not receive it.
- Tracking shows only what was measured. A message sent without tracking has nothing there.
