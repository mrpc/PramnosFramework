## A person's card in the assistants' control centre: everything held for them, and their conversations deleted for good

### Added

- `Ai/all?person=` lists, below what the companion knows of the person, everything else the
  assistant holds for them. Each section is folded, counted, and left out when empty:
  - routines, reminders and timers;
  - calendar;
  - the artifacts they made and those shared with them;
  - shares, questions and dossier;
  - their own skills.

  `Analyst\Overview::holdingsOf()` gives it, and `AiController::personSections()` adds an
  application's own sections.
- The card's conversations can be selected and deleted for good, after a folded confirmation.
  Each deletion is audited as `ai.conversation_deleted`. It is root's by default:
  `AiController::deletesConversations()`.

### Fixed

- Deleting a conversation also deletes its questions (`assist.chat_asks`) and the members of the
  artifacts it made, which were left behind before.
