## A person's card in the assistants' control centre: everything held for them, and their conversations deleted for good

### Added

- `Ai/all?person=` lists, below what the companion knows of the person, everything else the
  assistant holds for them. Each section is folded, counted, and left out when empty:
  - routines, reminders and timers;
  - calendar;
  - the artifacts they made and those shared with them;
  - shares, questions and dossier;
  - their own skills.

  `Analyst\Overview::holdingsOf()` gives it, and `AiController::personSections()` adds an
  application's own sections.
- The card's conversations can be selected and deleted for good, after a folded confirmation.
  Each deletion is audited as `ai.conversation_deleted`. It is root's by default:
  `AiController::deletesConversations()`.

### Fixed

- Deleting a conversation also deletes its questions (`assist.chat_asks`) and the members of the
  artifacts it made, which were left behind before.

## The person's card in Greek, and saying what was deleted

### Fixed

- The person's card in the assistants' control centre is translated into Greek: its sections,
  their columns, and the deletion with its confirmation.
- The card shows the flash itself, so "2 conversations deleted." is seen under an application's
  own admin header that does not show it.

## On the person's card, everything held opens and is deleted where it can be

### Added

- An artifact's title on the card opens it read only, in the same tab and inside the
  administration, at `Ai/artifact/{id}`. The page shows the trail back to the card and a print
  form (`?print=1`), and each opening is audited as `ai.artifact_viewed`. A share's title opens what was shared.
- A *From* column says which conversation a row came from, or that it was made outside one.
  Each row puts its title first, then its date.
- Root deletes, by section, the routines, calendar items, artifacts, shares, dossier entries,
  skills and memories (`Ai/holdingsdelete`). Each deletion goes through what owns the row and is
  audited.

## An answer that says it was done, when no tool did it, is corrected

### Added

- An answer that called no tool, in a turn with tools, is read on the cheapest model. Where it
  tells the person something was done or arranged (a reminder, a save, a message sent), the model
  is sent back once to do it, or to say it was not done. This catches what the reminder pattern
  did not, such as «Κανονίστηκε — θα σου χτυπήσει υπενθύμιση στις 9:15» with no call.
- The check's cost counts in the turn, and a corrected message keeps `corrected: claimed`.
- It is on by default. `'chat' => ['verify_claims' => false]` turns it off, and a callable
  decides it per assistant.

## An artifact opened from a person's card inside the administration

### Changed

- `Ai/artifact/{id}` opens in the same tab and inside the administration's template. It shows
  the trail back to the person's card, a *Back to the card* button, and *Print or save as PDF*,
  which opens the bare print form (`?print=1`).

## The bell: read on sight, many as one, and a page that settles what it shows

### Added

- `NotificationBell::render(['readOnView' => 1200])`: an unread notification that stays in sight
  of the open list that long is read. It is off by default.
- `'notifications' => ['collapse' => ['todo' => ['title' => 'You have %d to-dos', 'url' => …,
  'min' => 2]]]` shows a group's unread as one item in the bell, and reading it reads them all.
  `POST /Notifications/read` takes several ids, and `Feed::list()` takes `collapse`.
- `data-pf-settles="todo:42"` on a page settles the signed-in person's notifications about what
  it shows, through `POST /Notifications/settle`, without a call in each controller.

## Learning keeps who said what, and keeps nothing of a story

### Fixed

- Memory learning kept the assistant's description of itself («…a small robot like me?») as a
  fact about the person, and the plot of a story made up together as one too. The learning call
  is now told:
  - a fact about the person comes from their own words alone, never from the assistant's reply;
  - what the assistant said of itself is only ever `self`;
  - a story, a game or role-play is about nobody (`Memory::PRETEND`). The profile's proposals
    come from the same call and follow the same rules.

## Repeating to-dos: the next opens when one is done

### Added

- `Todos::add()` and `update()` take `repeat`: `daily`, `weekdays`, `weekly`, `monthly` or
  `yearly`, and `''` stops it. A repeat needs a day or a reminder.
- When a repeating to-do is done, the next opens at once with the same title, link, assignee,
  privacy and repeat. Its reminder is at the next occurrence and its day moves as many days, and
  nobody is told it is theirs. Missed occurrences are not each copied, and dismissing or
  deleting one stops it.
- `Todos::nextOccurrence()` gives the next occurrence: at the same time of day in the
  organisation's zone, and for a month or a year from the first day, kept where the month has it.
- A sent reminder keeps its time in `reminded_at`.
- The to-do page's form, `TodosApi` and the Ask's to-do tool take `repeat`.
- A migration adds `repeat`, `repeat_from` and `reminded_at` to `pramnos.todos`. Run `migrate`.

## A partition's morning and diary: its own name, its own mark, told the application's way

### Added

- The morning's thought and the diary call the person by the application's assistant's
  `personName(Scope $scope): ?string` where it has one, else by the account's first name.
- An assistant with `tellMorning(Scope $scope, object $notice): bool` tells the morning its own
  way: whom and how, or nobody.

### Fixed

- The morning's once-a-day mark is per stored space. Before, one partition's morning stopped
  another's being told on the same day.

## A quiet source's message is not activity

### Changed

- `Inbox::activity()` leaves out the messages of `quiet_sources`, as `unreads()` does. They had
  been listed as their own kind (`todo`), which a page that did not know that kind drew as
  broken text. A reminder stays in its conversation.

## To-dos: in order by hand, one person's list, and their pictures

### Added

- `Todos::reorder($ids, $by)` puts items in the order given, and `POST /admin/Todo/reorder`
  takes `ids[]`.
- `Todos::list(…, person: $id)` gives one person's list: their items, nobody's, and what they
  gave away in the last 24 hours (`Todos::GIVEN_HOURS`).
- `'todos' => ['photo' => fn (int $user): ?string]` shows a person's picture beside their name on
  the to-do page.

## Activity seen, kept on the server

### Added

- When a person last saw their activity (mentions, reactions, replies) is kept on the server
  (`Inbox::activitySeen()`, `sawActivity()`), so every device agrees.
- `POST {conversations}/activityseen` moves it and tells their other devices (`activity.seen`).
- `GET activity` answers `seenAt` and `unseen`, and `GET unreads` answers `activityUnseen` for a
  badge (`Inbox::unseenActivity()`).

## The application's own fields on `{chat}/me`

### Added

- An assistant with `meExtras(Scope $scope): array` adds its fields, such as a photo, to
  `GET {chat}/me`. They never replace the framework's own.

## Notifications: a switch per type, and reading what is in sight a person's own choice

### Added

- `'notifications' => ['types' => [Class::class => 'label']]` names the types a person may turn
  off. A type turned off is not sent to them by any channel (`Notification\Preferences`).
- Whether the bell marks as read what stays in sight is each person's choice, after
  `read_on_view` milliseconds (500 by default).
- `GET`/`POST /Notifications/preferences` reads and saves the choices, and
  `Html\NotificationPreferences::render($back)` is the form for an account page.

## To-dos: exact times from sources, the giver told, a way per recipient, a piece at a time

### Added

- A source gives `due_at` (an exact time) and `remind_at`, kept in step like the day, and
  `Todos::add()` takes `due_at` too.
- `todos.url` as `fn (array $item, ?int $recipient)` leads each recipient their own way.
- Each piece of the list turns off on its own with `'todos' => ['tools' | 'menu' | 'dashboard' |
  'rules' | 'chat' => false]` (`Todos::offers()`), for an application moving onto it.

### Changed

- One's own item given to somebody else tells the giver when it is closed, as an `assigned` one
  does.
- `Todos::about($path)` finds an item linked with a query on that path. Asked with a query, it
  finds only that query.

## A partition's own persona, settings and first meetings

### Added

- An assistant with `chosenPersona(Scope)` gives a partition its own persona
  (`Personas::ofPartition()`). With `choosePersona(Scope, $key)`, `PUT {chat}/persona` in a
  partition keeps the choice the application's way, or refuses it with a 403.
- `Settings::of()` and `change()` take a partition: its own settings over the holder's. The
  voice and the style do too (`Speech::chosen/choose()`, `Acts::style/choose()`), as do the
  voice and pose endpoints.

### Changed

- `People::firstMeetings($userId, $partition)`, and `first_meetings` on `{chat}/me`, count only
  the conversations of that partition, or only the holder's.

## Each device told of its own profile

### Changed

- A device subscribed to push from a partition is tagged `chat@<partition>`, and moves when it
  subscribes again from another profile.
- A partition's notices (`TellsItsApp::appOf()`) reach only its devices. The holder's reach
  only the devices of no partition, and earlier subscriptions are the holder's. The senders
  (morning, routines, calendar, shares) pass the stored space, partition included.

## Notifications that must be acknowledged

### Added

- A notification whose `toDatabase()` returns `'requires_ack' => true` is read only by a click
  on it. "Mark all read" leaves it, being in sight does not read it, and a collapsed item does
  not fold it in. The feed marks it `ack`, and the bell draws it with a red mark.

## Tell me again later

### Added

- A notification put off (`Feed::snooze()`, `POST /Notifications/snooze`) is out of the bell
  and its counts until its moment, and comes back unread. The bell offers an hour, three hours
  and tomorrow at nine on each unread item.
- A migration adds `snoozed_until` to `notifications`. Run `migrate`.

## Why a notification is here, and acting on it from the bell

### Added

- Each item can say why it is there: its own `why`, or the label of a type the person may turn
  off, linked to `notifications.preferences_url`.
- `actions` in a notification's data (label, a path on this site, style) are buttons in the
  bell. They post with the page's token and then read the notification, without leaving the
  page.

## Quiet hours for notifications

### Added

- Each person's quiet hours (`Preferences::setQuiet()`, on the preferences form and endpoint),
  on their clock. In them a push and an email wait, and the bell keeps the notification. One
  that must be acknowledged goes through.

## The notifications' history, narrowed by type and days

### Added

- `Feed::list(…, filter: ['type' => …, 'from' => 'Y-m-d', 'to' => 'Y-m-d'])` and
  `GET /Notifications?type=&from=&to=` narrow the history. `Feed::typesSeen()` (`&types=1`)
  lists the kinds a person has had, for a dropdown.

## Skills withheld from an audience, and pushes the application holds back

### Added

- `audiences.<name>.skills_without` names skills an audience is not offered at all, `web` among
  them. Their tools are not loaded, and `use_skill` answers "not available here".
- The application's assistant's `notifies(Scope $scope, string $kind, int $at): bool` is asked
  before each of the assistant's pushes (`morning`, `reminder`, `calendar`, `answer`, `share`).
  False holds the push back, and the rest stays in the app. `RoutineReady::tag()` takes the kind.

## The weather, a skill beside the web

### Added

- `weather(place, days)` in the `weather` skill (`Llm\Weather\Weather`): MET Norway's forecast,
  a place by name through Nominatim or the application's own `place` and `here`, summed up by
  day on the person's clock, with its sources named.
- It is on with `'llm' => ['chat' => ['weather' => ['user_agent' => …]]]`. Forecasts are kept until
  they expire and asked again with `If-Modified-Since`, and places are kept for 30 days.

## A number the figures do not bear out is answered again

A reporting answer gave a number that none of its own tool results made: no value, total, sum or
difference. The person then read it as a fact. An answer is now held to its figures before it is
read.

### Added

- `'numeric' => true` on a tool (or `numeric(): bool` on an MCP tool): every number the answer
  gives must follow from what such a tool returned. That means a value, a list's or column's
  total, a sum or difference of two, or a percentage. A number that does not is said back once
  (`Client::FIGURES`) and the answer is written again. One still not borne out is named under
  the answer (`Client::UNTRACED`).
- `Llm\Figures::untraced()`: the check itself. It reads Greek and English number formats, to the
  precision written, and leaves out dates, years, list numbering and the question's own numbers.
- The analyst's `metrics`, `visits`, `llm_spend` and `sql` are marked (`Analyst::FIGURES`). The
  result of `askWithTools()` carries `figures` and `untraced`, and a conversation's message keeps
  `corrected: figures`.
- Follow-ups are checked too. What numeric tools returned is kept on the answer (`figures_read`),
  and the conversation hands it to the next question (`Client::withFigures()`). An answer that
  calls no tool is held to those figures once it gives one of them. A number the person repeats
  that the figures do not bear out is pointed out to the model before it answers
  (`Client::DOUBTED`), so the model corrects it instead of building on it.

## The activity badge leaves out what was read in its conversation

The activity badge counted everything since the activity was last opened. A mention read in its
channel kept the badge, so people were told of things they had already seen.

### Fixed

- `Inbox::unseenActivity()`, and the `unseen` of `activity` and the `activityUnseen` of `unreads`,
  count a mention or a thread reply only when it is still unread in its conversation, past the
  person's read point there. A reaction has no read point and counts until the activity is seen,
  as before. The activity list itself is unchanged. A page that shows the badge fetches `unreads`
  again on `conversation.read`.
