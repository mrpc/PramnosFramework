## The rule editor offers templates and lists, and wears the theme

### Changed

- The email action's **template** and **unsubscribe list** are choices in the rule editor: the
  mail templates by title, and the declared mail types' lists (FW-234).
- **`Rules\Actions::templates($options, $fill)`**: an application whose templates are not the
  framework's `mailtemplates` says which it has and fills one for a person.
- The rule editor's controls take the theme's field classes, and its rows line up.

### Fixed

- The rule page's forms carry the session's token where the write-action contract looks for it.

### Documentation

- Audience guide: Templates, under Actions.
