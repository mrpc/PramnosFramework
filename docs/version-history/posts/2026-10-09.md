## A grant for an application speaks that application's vocabulary

An application declares its resources, scopes and condition keys with
`PUT /api/internal/clients/{client_id}/capabilities`, and the only reader of that declaration
was the application's own page. The permission form took *Object type* and *Action* as free
text, so `invoice` for `invoices`, or `View invoices` for `read`, was stored and matched nothing,
with no message.

### Added

- `CapabilitiesSyncService::catalog()` — every application's declared resources, scopes and
  conditions, active and withdrawn; `vocabulary()` — the active part, for a form;
  `problemWith()` / `problemsIn()` — why a grant cannot match its application's declaration.
- The permission form: with an *Application ID* that declared capabilities, *Object type*
  offers its active resources, *Action* the chosen resource's scopes plus `*`, and *Conditions*
  lists the condition keys with their value types.
- The permissions list and a role's page mark a grant whose resource or scope its application no
  longer declares, or never did, with the reason.

### Changed

- Saving a grant for such an application with an undeclared object type or action is refused,
  with the reason. No application, or one that declared nothing: free text, as before.
- `init` with the `authserver` feature routes `PUT`/`POST /api/internal/clients/{clientId}/capabilities`
  and `GET /api/internal/permissions` outside the version prefix, at the paths the integration
  guide publishes, with an `InternalPermissions` API wrapper. It no longer writes
  `POST /api/<version>/capabilities/sync[/{clientId}]` or puts it in the OpenAPI overrides. An
  existing project's `routes.php` is its own; the guide shows the group to add.

### Documentation

- AuthServer Integration Guide §5: the example authenticates with HTTP Basic
  (`client_id:client_secret`), as the text and the controller do — it showed a Bearer token. The
  section's history notes are replaced by what holds now.
- Authorization Guide, *The Permissions screen*: the vocabulary, the refusal and the marks.
- Upgrade Guide: the refusal, and republishing the `permissions` and `roles` views.
