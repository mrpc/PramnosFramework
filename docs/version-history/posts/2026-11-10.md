## The administration's screens under the API, for a SPA of a part of it

### Added

- **`ScreenApi::mount($r, $path, Screen::class)`** puts an administration screen's actions
  under the API, run through the screen's own `exec()`:
  - `GET {path}` is the list, `GET {path}/{action}/{id?}` a read, `POST {path}/{action}/{id?}`
    a write.
  - The floor, the ability, `auth()` and the screen's own checks hold.
  - A read answers the data its view was given. A write answers its messages and where the page
    would go next. The screen's errors are a 422, in its own words.
- **`AdminApi::routes($r)`** mounts the framework's screens under `/admin/{name}`, every one or
  some (`['rules', 'segments']`). It is opt-in: `init` writes it as a commented line, because
  an administrator's token then reaches the administration too.
- **`Controller::answerTheApi()`** is the mode behind it. `redirect()`, a refusal and the view's
  `display()` end the action with a `ScreenAnswer` instead of a page. An application's screen
  is mounted the same way, with no change of its own.

### Documentation

- Six screen actions that had no docblock now have one, so every route says what it does in the
  API's document.

  See the [API guide](../../Pramnos_API_Guide.md#the-administrations-screens-under-the-api).

## A conversation summed up by the analyst, for its reader

### Added

- **`Summary::of($conversationId, $reader, $since, $language)`** sums up what a reader has not
  read, the last hours, or what came since a time. It answers markdown citing `[#id]`, the
  cited ids, `from`, `to`, `count` and `cut`.
  - It reads only what the reader may read: threads in, deleted messages out.
  - The newest 400 messages and 60,000 characters go to the model.
  - Nothing to sum up is an empty answer with no call.
  - The call is costed to the reader, and the summary is kept nowhere.
- **`summarize/{id}`** on the conversations' screen and API (POST: `since`, `hours`,
  `language`).
- **`conversation_summary`**, the analyst's tool for the same, with the same rights.

  See the [Conversations guide](../../Pramnos_Conversations_Guide.md#a-summary).
