## Conversations notify: the bell and a push, as Slack's, with "Notify anyway"

### Added

- A new message now notifies — in the bell, and as a push to every browser the person subscribed
  (`Pramnos\Conversations\Notify`, `ConversationNotification`): the other members of a direct or
  group conversation, whoever it mentions (`<@id>` or a handle), whoever is in the thread a reply
  is in, and — where their setting for the conversation is `all` — the rest of its members. Each
  member's setting (`all`, `mentions`, `none`) and mute is kept; its writer and anybody the
  application's `quiet` hook pauses are not notified. The pushes go out in the queue
  (`conversation_notify`), so a writer never waits for fifty of them.
- "Notify anyway": `post/{id}` answers `paused` — who the message addressed with their
  notifications paused — and its writer may `nudge/{messageId}` one of them, once.
- Reading a conversation reads its notifications in the bell.
- `pf-push-worker.js`: `push` and `notificationclick` handlers for a service worker of the
  application's own, by `importScripts()` — the conversations installed as an app have one, and it
  had no `push` handler, so no notification could ever show on a phone.
- Settings: `conversations.page` (where a notification opens), `conversations.notify_via`,
  `conversations.notification` (a class of the application's own).
- A channel's new member starts at `mentions` — notified of what concerns them, as Slack's
  default — and a direct, group or bot conversation's at `all`. Members already in a channel keep
  the `all` they were given; an installation that wants them at the new default runs once
  `UPDATE conversation_members m SET notify = 'mentions' FROM conversations c WHERE c.conversationid = m.conversationid AND c.kind IN ('channel', 'private', 'system') AND m.notify = 'all'`
  (PostgreSQL; on MySQL as a join).

### Why

Messages reached only whoever had the conversations open: `Messages::post()` fired
`MessagePosted` and `MentionReceived`, and nothing listened to either for a notification.

See [Conversations — Notifications](../../Pramnos_Conversations_Guide.md#notifications-the-bell-and-a-push).

## Conversations: who may use `@channel`, `@everyone` and the other handles

### Added

- `conversations.handles_may`: who may use a handle — `here`, `channel`, `everyone`,
  `group:<name>` or `groups` — as a usertype floor or a callable given the writer and the
  conversation. A conversation's owner may always use `@here` and `@channel` in it.
- A handle its writer may not use stays in the message as text and tells nobody, as Slack does;
  `messages/{id}` offers its reader's composer only the handles they may use
  (`Handles::offered($id, $reader)`, `Handles::may()`, `Handles::permitted()`).

### Why

Anybody who could write in the general conversation could notify the whole team with `@everyone`.

See [Conversations — Handles](../../Pramnos_Conversations_Guide.md#messages).

## Conversations: scheduled messages

### Added

- `Pramnos\Conversations\Scheduled`: a message written to be sent later — `create()`, `mine()`,
  `change()`, `cancel()` — sent at its time by `conversations:send-scheduled`, which the framework
  runs every minute, as its writer and once. One whose writer has left the conversation is not
  sent; it stays, with why, until it is changed.
- HTTP: `schedule/{conversationId}`, `scheduled`, `reschedule/{id}`, `unschedule/{id}`.
- Migration `2026_11_07_000001_create_conversation_scheduled_table` (`conversation_scheduled`,
  declared personal data): state only — a row goes when its message is sent.

See [Conversations — Scheduled messages](../../Pramnos_Conversations_Guide.md#scheduled-messages).

## Conversations: drafts on the server, on every device

### Added

- `Pramnos\Conversations\Drafts`: what somebody has begun writing in a conversation or a thread,
  kept on the server — `save()`, `get()`, `mine()` — and let go of when they send a message there.
  Each change is told on their own channel as `conversation.draft`.
- HTTP: `drafts`, `draft/{conversationId}`; `messages/{id}` answers `draft` too.
- Migration `2026_11_07_000002_create_conversation_drafts_table` (`conversation_drafts`, declared
  personal data). A conversation still opens, with no draft, before it is run.

### Why

Drafts lived in each browser's `localStorage`: what was begun on the computer was not on the phone.

See [Conversations — Drafts](../../Pramnos_Conversations_Guide.md#drafts).

## Conversations: a worker or the scheduler reads the administration's settings

### Fixed

- A queue worker runs as whichever application started it — usually the site's, whose
  configuration has no `conversations` block. Its notifications of a message ignored the `quiet`
  hook, so somebody paused was notified anyway, and opened at the site's `/Conversations` instead
  of the administration's. Every `conversations` setting is now read through
  `Conversations::settings()`: the running application's block, or `app/admin.php`'s where it has
  none. The same held for the scheduled messages `conversations:send-scheduled` posts, and for
  link previews and the files' path in the worker.
- Where a notification opens is worked out by the request that wrote the message and carried in
  the `conversation_notify` task (`Notify::page()`).
