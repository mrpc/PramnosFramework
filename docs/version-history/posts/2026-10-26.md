## The rich editor: the toolbar and its panel share one sticky wrapper, panels can float under the selected block, and a site's own picture actions hang off a hook instead of a copied file

### Fixed

- The toolbar alone was sticky; the link/picture panel under it was not, so scrolling a long article put an open panel's Address bar off-screen the moment a link or picture deep in the text was edited. The toolbar and the panel now share one sticky wrapper, `.pf-rich-head`.

### Added

- `panels: 'docked' | 'floating'` on `createRichEditor()` (`data-pf-rich-panels` for one field, `'panels' => 'floating'` in `messaging.rich` for the application, `panels` for the Svelte component): floating puts the link and picture panels over the text, just under the block they are about, instead of docked under the toolbar — useful where the toolbar sits far from a long article's middle.
- `pictureActions` on `createRichEditor()`: a list of `{ label, run({ node, at, editor, upload }) }`, drawn as buttons on the picture panel after its address. A site's own picture editing — cropping, filters, swapping a picture already in the text — hangs off this instead of a change to the copied core file.
- A docblock note at the top of `core.js` ("FRAMEWORK-OWNED, refreshed by every `project:resync`"), matching the one already on `lib/debug.js` and other framework-owned project files.
- Reported by glideday: three changes written straight into a project's copy of `core.js` were overwritten by the next resync. The sticky wrapper was a bug fix for every project and moved into the core as it was; the panel placement and the picture actions became the two options/hooks above, so the same result no longer needs editing the copy.

### Documentation

- `docs/Pramnos_Rich_Editor_Guide.md`: the `panels` option and attribute, and a `pictureActions` example under *Adding a site's own*.
