## The DevPanel's policy callback decides the development route only

### Documentation

- A policy callback set by a `DevPanelController` subclass replaces the development route's
  usertype floor, and nothing else. An account `app.php` admits on any deployment (the `root`
  role, `usertypes`, `userids`) is let in before the callback is asked, so a callback that says
  no does not keep root out.
    - This was always the behaviour. It is now stated in the DevPanel Guide and on the property.
    - To keep an account out, narrow the lists.

## `schedule_task` requires a kind

### Fixed

- **A reminder no longer becomes a question.** Asked "remind me in 2 minutes: …", a model called
  `schedule_task` without `kind`. The task was made as `ask`, and two minutes later a model was
  asked the words instead of the person being reminded of them.
    - `kind` is now required in the tool's schema, and a call without it is refused with an error
      that says which kind is which.
    - The model then calls again with the kind.

## A page left open refreshes its CSRF tokens

### Added

- **`pf-utils.js` refreshes a page's CSRF tokens once they are ten minutes old**, when the tab
  comes back into view and before a form carrying one is sent.
    - It updates every `_csrf_token` field, `<meta name="csrf">`, and every form-token field.
    - A form signed in on another tab, or one left open past its session, is no longer refused
      with what was typed lost.
    - There is no timer, so an idle session still expires.
- **`GET /Csrf`** (`Pramnos\Application\Controllers\Csrf`) answers the current tokens. It is
  served by the framework and never cached, and refuses a cross-site request.
- **`<meta name="csrf-refresh">`** on every page names it.
- **`window.pfRefreshCsrf()`** asks at once.
- **`pramnos-testing`'s `Event`** has `stopImmediatePropagation()`.

### Documentation

- The Security Guide has a new section, **A page left open: the token is refreshed**.
- There is a new row in the Upgrade Guide.

## Dashboard cards: four widths, settings, and drawn one at a time

FW-130, items 1–3.

### Added

- **Four widths on a grid of four columns:** `s`, `m`, `l` and `full` (`Widget::SMALL`, `MEDIUM`,
  `LARGE`, `FULL`, `Widget::SIZES`). The grid has two columns on a tablet and one on a phone. The
  width button goes round the four.
- **Settings per card.**
    - `Widget::options()` declares what may be set: a list of values, or a function that cleans a
      free one.
    - `setting()` reads a choice, and `configure()` hands a widget its settings.
    - `Widget::cleanSettings()` is the cleaning on its own.
    - Each person's choices are kept in their layout (`{id, size, settings}`) and cleaned against
      the declaration. The card's gear opens a form of them.
- **`Widget::description()`**, shown as the hint on each card offered.
- **`GET …/Dashboard/card?id=…`** (`DashboardController::card()`) answers one card's HTML with
  this person's width and settings, or 404.
    - `pf-utils.js` uses it to put an added card, or one whose settings changed, in place without
      a reload, and draws its charts.
- **`dashboard/card.html.php`** in the three themes. The dashboard draws every card through it.

### Changed

- **A widget's default width is `m`.** A layout saved with `half` reads as `m`, and
  `Widget::HALF` remains as an alias.
- **Layouts carry `settings`**, and `Layouts::for()` and `clean()` answer them for every card.

### Documentation

- The Dashboard Guide covers widths, a new **Settings** section and the one-card endpoint.
- There is a new row in the Upgrade Guide.

## A reminder asked for in a new conversation leads back to it

### Fixed

- **A reminder asked for in the first question of a conversation now links to it.**
    - Before, it was kept with no conversation, because that conversation exists only once the
      question is answered. Its notification therefore led to the chat page in general.
    - Now the tasks the scheduling tools made during the answer are given the new conversation's
      id as soon as it is stored (`Routines::linkMade()`).

## Dashboard cards are dragged and resized with a finger as with a mouse

FW-130, item 5, ported from an application's own admin theme.

### Changed

- **Pointer events, in place of HTML5 drag and drop.** HTML5 drag and drop does not work with
  touch and cannot resize.
    - A card is dragged by its grip (`data-pf-widget-grip`). It follows the pointer, with a
      placeholder of its size where it will land, and the window scrolls near its edges.
    - It is resized by its corner (`data-pf-widget-size-handle`), column by column, with the width
      shown on it.
    - The arithmetic is on `window.pfDashboard`.
- **`dashboard/card.html.php`** has the grip and the corner in the three themes, with their styles
  in each `components.css`.

### Documentation

- The Dashboard Guide describes dragging and resizing.

## A page for scheduled AI tasks and shared reports

Phase four of scheduled AI tasks, for applications without a SPA.

### Added

- **`/chat/scheduled`** (`ChatPageController::scheduled()`), in the three themes.
    - It lists a person's tasks, with run now, pause, resume, delete and their runs.
    - It lists the reports shared with them, with receive and stop.
    - It shows a task's runs, a report's with *Ask about it*.
    - It has a form to schedule any kind, with the "for whom" choice only for somebody the policy
      lets share.
    - The conversations page links to it.
- **Its POST actions:** `schedule`, `scheduledupdate`, `scheduleddelete`, `scheduledrun` and
  `scheduledsubscribe`.

### Fixed

- **A one-off scheduled decades ahead no longer fails the insert.** A MySQL `TIMESTAMP` ends in
  2038. Such a task is now refused with a sentence, and one more than ten years ahead is treated as
  a typo.
- **`run_at` accepts `2026-10-16T17:00`**, as a `datetime-local` field sends it, beside
  `2026-10-16 17:00`.

### Documentation

- The LLM Guide has a new subsection, **The scheduled page**.
- There is a new row in the Upgrade Guide.

## The SPA's Scheduled screen: every kind and cadence, runs, shared reports

### Changed

- **`components/llm/Scheduled.svelte`** schedules questions, reminders and reports, with every
  cadence: once, daily, weekly, monthly, and a weekday of the month. A report offers whom it is
  for and when readers are told.
- **It shows a task's runs.** A report's run opens as a conversation of the reader's own
  (`continueRun`, then `onopen`).
- **It lists the reports shared with this person**, to receive or stop.
- **`lib/llm/chat.js`** has `routineRuns`, `run`, `continueRun`, `reports`, `subscribe` and
  `unsubscribe`, and the paths `runs` and `reports`.
- **`GET /chat/routines`** also answers `kinds`, and the `owners` this person may use.
- **The link reads "Scheduled"**, in place of "Scheduled reports".

Projects take the screen with `project:resync --spa-components`.

## Deleting an account hands its tasks over and erases what the assistant kept

Phase four of scheduled AI tasks, completed.

### Added

- **The delete-account page asks who takes over the person's scheduled tasks**, organisation by
  organisation (`OAuth2/delete_account_tasks.html.php` in the three themes).
    - Reports the organisation receives are proposed for a successor.
    - The person's own tasks go only if they tick it.
    - With nobody to take them, they are deleted.
    - The hand-over runs before the erase.
- **`Routines::handoverChoices()` and `handover()`**, and **`TaskPolicy::successors()`**, which
  defaults to the organisation's other active members.
- **The LLM Guide has an example for an application's own member-removal flow.**

### Fixed

- **Erasing an account now erases what the assistant kept.** Before, `AccountErasure` left all of
  this behind:
    - the person's conversations and every message in them;
    - the assistant's memory of them, their queued questions and their agreements;
    - their dashboard;
    - their scheduled tasks, with each run's record and each reader's subscription, and their
      own subscriptions.
- **The call log keeps the cost and loses the person:** `userid`, prompt, messages and reply are
  cleared.
- **The page no longer fails where the installation keeps no organisations.** An organisation's
  name falls back to its number.

### Documentation

- The Authentication Guide's table of what the erase deletes has the assistant's tables.
- The LLM Guide covers asking who takes over, with an example.
- There is a new row in the Upgrade Guide.

## The dashboard's catalogue, fields, and figures only when read

FW-133, from an application now running on the framework's dashboard.

### Added

- **A catalogue to add cards from.** It is a dialog in the three themes, searched, with each
  card's description.
    - A card on the board is marked as such.
    - A card hidden is offered again without a reload.
    - `pf-utils.js` also accepts `[data-pf-dashboard-add="id"]` buttons.
- **Fields for a card's settings.** An option can be `['label', 'input' => text|date|number,
  'clean', 'when']` or `['label', 'values']`.
    - Labels are translated.
    - Dates are date fields.
    - A field waits for the setting it is shown for, and is not sent while hidden.
    - `Widget::field()` describes an option, and `cardFor()` hands the view `fields`.
- **`DashboardController::dashboardHome()`.** "Back to the default" returns there and says so.
- **The script's words come from the page**, through the board's `data-pf-dashboard-say`.

### Changed

- **The dashboard no longer works out figures nobody reads.** The four figures handed to a view
  of the older shape are `Dashboard\Widgets\LazyFigures`, worked out on first read, so the health
  checks no longer run on every load.
- **`$this->catalogue` holds every card this person may have**, each with `placed`, not only the
  ones they may add.
- **A select's placed options are hidden rather than removed.**
